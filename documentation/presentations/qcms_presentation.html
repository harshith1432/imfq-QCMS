What is QCMS Enterprise OS?

The foundation for institutional quality: standardizing daily execution and continuous Kaizen.

QCMS Enterprise
★
QCMS is the enterprise operating system for managing Quality Circles, 8D Problem Solving, and Six Sigma projects. It bridges the gap between shopfloor operators, facilitators, and executive management.

1. Standardize (8 Stages)

Enforce rigorous 8D workflows: Problem Definition (5W2H) → Observation → Cause Identification → Root Cause Validation → Countermeasures → Verification → Standardization → Team Recognition.

2. Automate (7 QC Tools)

Instant auto-generation of Pareto Charts, Ishikawa Fishbone, Before/After Histograms, Control Charts (SPC), Scatter Correlation, Checksheets, and Stratification.

3. Govern (Review Gates)

Multi-tier signoff workflows, stage-level revision requests, immutable audit logs, facilitator assistance channels, and executive ROI dashboards.

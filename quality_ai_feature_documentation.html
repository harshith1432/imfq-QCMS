💡 Why is Quality AI Safe and Accurate?
Unlike public chatbots (like ChatGPT) that guess or hallucinate answers, Quality AI queries your actual organization's verified quality records, live plant metrics, and standard QC methodologies. It never shares your company's proprietary data outside your tenant.

2. Data Inflow: Where Does Quality AI Fetch Its Data?

Quality AI is not a generic language model. It acts as an intelligent aggregator fetching data from 4 distinct internal sources in real time:

Source 1 📊 Live Relational Database (Real-Time Organizational Data)
  • Workforce & Employees: Queries users and project_members to compute total staff, active members, and QC circle enrollment percentage.
  • Plant Facilities & Departments: Queries plants and departments to map which teams operate in which physical locations.
  • Project Portfolio & Growth: Queries projects to analyze stage distribution (how many projects are in Stage 1 vs Stage 4 vs Stage 8), running vs completed projects, and completion velocity.
  • Gamification Leaderboard: Queries employee_leaderboard and employee_points to highlight top-ranked quality contributors.
Source 2 📚 Historical Quality Knowledge Repository (RAG Vector Database)
  • Archived Problem Solutions: Stored in knowledge_repository containing verified root causes, 5-Why findings, and countermeasure plans from previous successful projects.
  • Dense Mathematical Vector Embeddings: Uses the all-MiniLM-L6-v2 natural language model to convert quality problems into 384-dimensional semantic vector arrays stored in PostgreSQL (pgvector).
  • Tangible Impact Metrics: Retrieves verified % KPI improvement and financial cost savings (₹) achieved in past projects.
Source 3 🏆 Standardized 8-Stage QC Methodology & 7 QC Tools Manual
  • 8-Stage DMAIC Framework: Stage 1 (Problem Definition) through Stage 8 (Standardization & Closure).
  • Statistical Quality Tools: Expert rules for Ishikawa Fishbone Diagrams (6M), Pareto 80/20 Analysis, SPC Control Charts (X-bar & R-chart), Check Sheets, Scatter Plots, and 3W1H Action Plans.
Source 4 🧭 System Navigation & 200+ How-To Configuration Guides
  • Step-by-step instructions on configuring plant locations, managing departments, importing members via CSV, customizing the Section 9 Sign-Off Hierarchy for PDF storyboards, and adjusting stage weightages.

Detailed Pipeline Steps:

Stage Component What Happens
1. Authentication rag_routes.py Extracts the encrypted JWT token from the request header, identifies the user ID, and locks all database queries strictly to that user's org_id.
2. Privacy Check QualityAIAssistant Checks the user's role. If a non-executive employee asks for sensitive financial ledger data, billing rates, or admin passwords, access is politely blocked with role explanations.
3. Intent Routing Pattern Classifier Classifies the user's query into one of 4 categories: Live Counts, Configuration How-To, Quality Methodology, or Historical RAG Search.
4. RAG Vector Match vector_service.py Encodes the query using all-MiniLM-L6-v2 and runs Cosine Similarity against historical project summaries to find the top matching past solutions.
5. Formatting & Links Markdown Engine Combines the answers with bold highlights, emoji bullet points, KPI stats, and clickable direct links to the relevant project storyboard.
